What I follow

  • 01
    Formula 1 Now

    Currently obsessed. Every race weekend, every qualifying lap, every team radio. Riding with Red Bull and Verstappen — the strategy calls, the politics, the mechanical artistry, the 1-second gap that rewrites everything. Chess at 340 km/h and I am completely gone for it.

    Motorsport
  • 02
    La Liga

    Where football philosophy actually lives. Technical, tactical, brutal in the best way. Spanish football made me understand the game differently.

    Football
  • 03
    UEFA Champions League

    The only club competition that still gives me actual nerves. Knockout football at its highest level is a different animal entirely.

    Football
  • 04
    FIFA World Cup

    Every four years, the whole world agrees on one thing. That shared attention — billions watching the same ninety minutes — is irreplaceable. Nothing else comes close.

    Football
  • 05
    ICC Cricket

    World Cups, Test matches, the works. Cricket is the sport I grew up arguing about at 11pm with strangers online. Still do. Never stop.

    Cricket
  • 06
    IPL — RCB ★★

    Royal Challengers Bengaluru. Two-time champions. I bleed red and gold every April. The IPL is the best cricket format ever invented and RCB is the most entertaining thing in it — always has been.

    Cricket · IPL
  • 07
    Olympics

    Thirty sports, two weeks, one feeling. Athletes who've spent their entire lives building to this single moment. It's impossible to not get invested in every last one of them.

    Multi-sport
