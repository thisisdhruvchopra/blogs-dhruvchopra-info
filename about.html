Dhruv Chopra

Kanpur, India · researcher by day, everything else by night

I've been interested in computers since nursery and built my own PC at ten. That probably explains most of what followed — and none of why I now think this hard about coffee.

By day I work in deception technology — building honeypots, studying how attackers behave inside artificial environments, and chasing a question I find genuinely fascinating: how long can you keep a sophisticated adversary inside a digital fiction before they realise it isn't real? If you want the work itself, it's all at dhruvchopra.info.

This site is for everything else. Astrophysics has been with me as long as computers have — the scale of things, the indifference of the cosmos, the sheer fact that we exist to ask about it. I travel solo when I can, five countries so far, and I'm convinced productive disorientation is the entire point of going. I love cinema the way I love good writing: for the compression. I have an astrophysicist's curiosity about perfume chemistry and a footballer's irrational emotional investment in results I have no control over.

I also drink too much coffee. I've accepted this about myself.

I'm targeting Masters programs in Germany for 2026/27, and slowly building toward video — thinking out loud on camera about the things I can't stop thinking about. The channel exists. The videos are coming.
