Dhruv Chopra · Music

Sound is
where I
go quiet.

I don't have a single genre. I have moods. Some nights it's Kishore Kumar at 2am, some mornings it's Max Richter before anyone else is awake. Music is the one thing I consume entirely without wanting to explain it.

Genres I live in

🎹 Neo-Classical · Ambient Max Richter, Nils Frahm, Ólafur Arnalds
🎷 Jazz Miles Davis, Bill Evans, Kamasi Washington
🎶 Hindi Film · Retro Kishore Kumar, R.D. Burman, Lata Mangeshkar
🎸 Alternative · Indie Radiohead, Bon Iver, Nick Drake
🎼 Film Scores Hans Zimmer, Jonny Greenwood, Ennio Morricone

Albums that stayed

Kind of Blue

Miles Davis · 1959

Six musicians making decisions in real time. Modal jazz that still doesn't sound like it was recorded sixty-five years ago.

On the Nature of Daylight

Max Richter · 2004

The track I've put on more times than I can count when the world feels too loud. Six minutes of pure, still composition.

In Rainbows

Radiohead · 2007

The record that made me understand what it means for an album to have a feeling rather than just songs.

Golden Era Hindi Film

Various · 1950s–80s

R.D. Burman understood rhythm in a way most Western producers still don't. This entire era is an education in feel.

Reminiscence

Nils Frahm · 2021

Piano music that sounds like thinking. Perfect for late nights with a screen.
