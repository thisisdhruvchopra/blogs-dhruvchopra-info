Things that genuinely move me

Light is a time machine

Look at Andromeda and you're seeing light that left 2.5 million years ago — before our species existed. The night sky is a fossil record.

~2.537 million light-years

You are literally stardust

Every heavy element in your body — carbon, iron, calcium — was forged in stellar cores and scattered by supernovae. Not a metaphor. Chemistry.

Stellar nucleosynthesis · 1957

The observable isn't the whole

We only see light that's had time to reach us in 13.8 billion years. Beyond that horizon: more universe. Possibly infinite. We can't know.

Cosmological horizon

The Pale Blue Dot

Sagan turned Voyager 1 around to photograph Earth from 6 billion km. A mote of dust in a sunbeam. Nothing has recalibrated me more.
