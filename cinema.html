Films I return to

1972 The Godfather Francis Ford Coppola The one film that gets better every time you understand more about power.
2014 Interstellar Christopher Nolan Astrophysics + cinema in one place. Of course it's on this list.
1968 2001: A Space Odyssey Stanley Kubrick The film that asked whether intelligence implies consciousness, in 1968.
1993 Schindler's List Steven Spielberg History and cinema colliding at maximum force. Unmissable.
2019 Parasite Bong Joon-ho The architecture as metaphor. The genre pivots. The ending. All of it.
2010 Inception Christopher Nolan The film that made me want to understand how films are actually built.

Directors I study

  • Stanley Kubrick Symmetry as control. Every frame a statement about power.
  • Christopher Nolan Time as narrative material. Structure as theme.
  • Bong Joon-ho Class as architecture. Genre as camouflage.
  • Alfonso Cuarón Long takes as emotional commitment. The camera as trust.
  • Denis Villeneuve Silence as language. Scale used with restraint.
