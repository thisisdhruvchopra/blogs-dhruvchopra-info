Rules I live by

  • I.
    Grind fresh, always. Pre-ground coffee is the audio equivalent of a heavily compressed MP3. Technically the same song. Not the same thing.
  • II.
    Water temperature is not optional. 93°C ± 2 for pour-over. Not boiling. Never boiling. This is not a minor detail.
  • III.
    Bad coffee in a great place still beats great coffee in a bad one. Context is a flavour. The thick, sweet sludge at a hole-in-the-wall in Bangkok at 7 a.m. was better than most specialty cups I've had. I can't explain this. I don't need to.
  • IV.
    Ratio is everything. 1:15 to 1:17 for filter. 1:2 for espresso. Going outside this range without knowing what you're doing is how mediocre coffee happens.
  • V.
    Instant coffee is not coffee. I said what I said.
