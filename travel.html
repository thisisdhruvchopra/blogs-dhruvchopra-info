🇹🇷 Turkey

Between

2023

Turkish breakfast — which alone would justify the flight. The Bosphorus at night, ferries crossing between Asia and Europe every half hour, like it's nothing.

🇻🇳 Vietnam

Ha Long
& The Lanterns

2024

Ha Long Bay from the water — limestone karsts rising from the sea like something out of a geology textbook you didn't expect to actually look this good. Hoi An at night, all paper lanterns and soft noise. Vietnamese coffee, which is a civilisation-level contribution to the world, and I stand by that.

🇮🇳 India · Home

The country
I keep
rediscovering

All my life · still going

I was born here and still manage to be surprised by it. India has a magnitude problem — it's too large to summarise and too varied to reduce. My best moments here have been in corners no one planned: small cities, late trains, roadside dhabas at 2am, fog-covered ghats, old bazaars that smell like every century at once.
