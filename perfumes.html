Dhruv Chopra · Perfumes

Scent is
memory made
chemical.

Top picks by occasion — because the right fragrance isn't the best one in the world, it's the right one for the room you're about to walk into.

01 Mon – Fri · 9 to 6

Office

Dior Sauvage EDP Woody · Aromatic Safe in the best sense — professional, confident, never distracting. Bergamot opens, ambroxan stays all day. Every meeting I've walked into wearing this has gone fine. 01
02 Evenings · after dark

Date Night

Tom Ford Oud Wood Oriental · Woody Rosewood, cardamom, rare oud. Rich and intimate without being loud — the most lived-in smell I know. Wear it when you want to be remembered. 01
Frederic Malle Portrait of a Lady Floral · Oriental Turkish rose, patchouli, incense. Technically a feminine fragrance. I don't care. It stops conversations and starts better ones. 02
03 Weekends · no agenda

Casual

Maison Margiela Replica Jazz Club Woody · Aromatic Rum, pink pepper, vetiver. Smells exactly like what it's named after — a late-night bar, warm and unhurried. The only fragrance that makes you want to slow down. 01
Byredo Bal d'Afrique Floral · Woody African marigold, musk, cedar. Warm and golden — wears like sunshine. The summer pick. Gets better as the day heats up. 02
04 Events · weddings · dinners

Formal

Amouage Reflection Man Aromatic · Floral Neroli, iris, sandalwood. Quietly the most elegant thing I own. Nothing announces itself — it just reveals itself, slowly, to people who get close enough to notice. 01
