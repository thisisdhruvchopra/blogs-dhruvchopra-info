Opinions I hold loudly

  • 🧠
    Pressing is everything. High press done correctly is the closest football comes to chess at speed. Gegenpressing changed how I watch the game permanently — once you see the trigger moments, you can't unsee them.
  • 📐
    Space, not the ball. The most interesting thing happening in any phase of play is usually off the ball. Full-backs in half-spaces. The striker pinning centre-backs. Movement that creates the passes you actually see.
  • ⏳
    Football is the only sport with genuine time. No timeouts. No commercial breaks engineering drama. Ninety minutes of continuous cause and effect, where a single moment of quality can undo an hour of structure. That compression is why it matters.
  • 🌍
    The World Cup is still the thing. Champions League football is technically superior. But there is nothing — nothing — like a World Cup knockout game at 1 a.m. with a billion people watching the same ninety minutes.
